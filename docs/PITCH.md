# Pitch script

At Ox Hack we had 60 seconds with one judge and 2 minutes with the others, and no
script, so the clever parts never got said. This time every line is written, timed and
owned. We rehearse twice with a phone timer before judging. If a judge interrupts, we
answer and jump to the next owned line, we do not restart.

Fill the brackets by hour 1. Keep sentences short; they are easier to say under pressure.

## The one sentence (say it first, every time)

> Unlike [what exists today], [project] [does what], which means [benefit the judge
> cares about].

## 3-minute version (stage, if there is one)

Same as 2-minute, plus a 30-second "how we built it" by Ajjar: the architecture in three
boxes, which parts are real and which are mocked, honestly.

## Rules while presenting

- Owned lines only. When your line ends, stop. The other person starts immediately.
- Never explain what you are about to show. Show it, then say one sentence about it.
- If something breaks, Teammate says "that is the live system, here is the recorded
  run" and plays the backup video. Record the backup video at 5:00.
- No "um, so basically". Start lines with a noun or a verb.
